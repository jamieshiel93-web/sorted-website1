When should I use this?

Use this form whenever something changes that could affect the way we support you.

  • Your contact details have changed.
  • You have moved or home access has changed.
  • Your usual schedule or availability is different.
  • Your household or pet arrangements have changed.
  • Something relevant to your support needs updating.

Need us to know quickly?

If the change affects an upcoming visit or needs prompt attention, mark it as urgent and contact Sorted. directly as well.
