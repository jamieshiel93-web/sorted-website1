Home Management

Practical, dependable support that helps your home remain organised, prepared and running smoothly.

View full details

Home Management is for the practical household jobs that need doing but can easily take up more time, organisation and headspace than they should.

What can be included

  • Changing bedding and preparing bedrooms
  • Laundry and ironing arrangements
  • Receiving household deliveries
  • Waiting in for tradespeople
  • Coordinating repair appointments
  • Restocking household essentials
  • Putting bins out and bringing them in
  • Watering indoor and outdoor plants
  • Preparing your home for visitors
  • Seasonal household tasks
  • General home checks
  • Light practical household assistance

Ideal for

  • Busy professionals
  • Families with demanding routines
  • Older adults living independently
  • People needing temporary practical support
  • Second-home owners
  • Clients preparing for guests or events

What a visit might look like

You might ask Sorted. to meet a plumber while you're at work, prepare bedding before family arrive, receive a delivery, restock household essentials or complete several practical jobs during one visit.

How it works

We agree your priorities and any important household instructions beforehand. Tasks are then completed during the agreed visit, with updates provided where useful.

Why clients use Home Management

It provides one dependable point of contact for the small but important jobs involved in keeping a household running — without you having to organise several different people yourself.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

A few useful things to know before arranging support with Sorted.

Can I combine more than one service?
Yes. Many bookings combine different kinds of support. A visit might include errands, paperwork, household jobs and organisation depending on what you need.
Do I have to book regular support?
No. Services can be arranged on a one-off or occasional basis. If you would like regular scheduled support, you can also explore our memberships.
How long is a typical visit?
Visit length depends on what needs to be completed. We will discuss the expected time and priorities before your booking.
Can my support change from one visit to another?
Yes. Sorted. is designed to be flexible. The jobs you need help with can change as your routine, circumstances or priorities change.
Can you help a relative who lives independently?
Yes, with their agreement. Sorted. can provide practical household help, errands, administration and companionship. We do not provide personal or medical care.
Can you accompany me to an appointment?
Accompanied appointments may be available by prior arrangement. We can discuss timings, transport and the kind of support you would like.
What happens during the complimentary consultation?
It is simply a friendly conversation about what you need help with, your routine and how Sorted. could make things easier. There is no obligation to proceed.
